Put your built Windows installer in this folder and name it exactly:

    Deka-Setup.exe

The Hero and CTA download buttons link to /downloads/Deka-Setup.exe.

Vite serves anything inside /public at the site root, so:
  public/downloads/Deka-Setup.exe   ->   https://yoursite.com/downloads/Deka-Setup.exe

If you change the filename, update WIN_DOWNLOAD in:
  src/components/Hero.jsx
  src/components/CTA.jsx
